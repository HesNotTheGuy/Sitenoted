# Sitenoted privacy policy

_Last updated: 29 August 2026_

Sitenoted does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.

## What it stores

The notes you write, and the settings you choose. Each note record holds its
text, colour, size and position, the hostname or URL you attached it to, and
timestamps. Deleted notes are kept for seven days so they can be restored, then
removed automatically.

## Where it is stored

In your own browser, through the `chrome.storage.local` API. Nothing is uploaded.
Sitenoted has no server, no account system, and makes no network requests
at all. It bundles no remote code and contacts no endpoint.
Notes made in an Incognito window are kept separate from your normal profile.

## What it can see

Sitenoted runs on the pages you visit so it can draw your notes there. It reads
the current page's hostname and path in order to decide which notes belong on
it. It does not read page content, form input, browsing history, or the URLs of
tabs you are not using, and it does not record where or when you browse.

## Who it shares with

Nobody. There are no third parties, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored. You can also delete
individual notes from a note's × button or the toolbar popup, or erase
everything at once from **Settings → Delete everything**. Export a backup first
if you want a copy.

## Contact

Report problems or ask questions through the extension's support link on its
Chrome Web Store listing.
